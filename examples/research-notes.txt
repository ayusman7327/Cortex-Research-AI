FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL STUDY

Title: Retrieval Methods for a Small Document Collection

Objective
Compare keyword retrieval and semantic retrieval on a collection of 200 short documents.

Method
The fictional trial used 100 manually written questions. The semantic system used normalized embeddings and cosine similarity.

Findings
The semantic retrieval trial achieved 92 percent accuracy. Keyword retrieval achieved 76 percent accuracy.

Limitations
The sample was small, English-only, and evaluated using manually written questions.

Future work
Evaluate more languages and a larger document collection.

These numbers are sample data for testing Cortex Research AI, not scientific evidence.
